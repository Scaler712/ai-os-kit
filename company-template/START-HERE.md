# Start here

This is your company folder. Your AI reads it before every job.

1. Copy the rules to the names your tools look for (once):
   `cp READ-FIRST.md AGENTS.md && cp READ-FIRST.md CLAUDE.md`
2. Fill in `core/`, starting with `core/clients.md`. Dictate, then tidy.
3. Paste 10 to 15 real emails you wrote into `communication/emails-i-sent.md`.
4. Open this folder in Claude Code, Codex or Hermes and ask: "what do you know about my company?"
   It should say what is filled in and point at the empty parts. If it invents facts, the rules file is not being read.
5. Follow the `install-the-ai-os` skill in `.claude/skills/` for the rest.

Back up the folder with git or a private sync. Never push it to a public place: it holds your business.
